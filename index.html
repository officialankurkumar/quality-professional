Building Quality Through Process Validation, Testing and Problem Solving

I am an Automotive and EV Quality Professional with 8+ years of experience in Supplier Quality, Customer Quality, New Product Development, Process Validation, Quality Audits and Continuous Improvement.

I have worked with different automotive products including steering systems, seating components, brake assemblies, EV motor control units and automotive lighting systems.

My experience includes supplier development, customer complaint handling, PPAP, APQP, process validation, root cause analysis, dimensional inspection, layout inspection and quality assurance activities.

I also have hands on experience with VMM, Contour Tracer, Roughness Tester, UTM, Basic CMM, Vernier Caliper, Micrometre, Height Gauge and other standard room measurement equipment.

I follow a simple approach to quality. Understand the process, check the results through data and testing, find the actual root cause and take effective corrective actions to prevent the problem from happening again.

My focus is on improving product quality, supporting suppliers and production teams, and delivering reliable products that meet customer requirements.
